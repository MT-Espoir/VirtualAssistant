TAI LIEU CHI TIET: CACH HE THONG AI HOAT DONG VA LUONG XU LY DU LIEU
Ngay cap nhat: 2026-04-16
Pham vi: Du an speech-to-text (Python voice assistant)

============================================================
1) TONG QUAN KIEN TRUC
============================================================
He thong duoc to chuc theo pipeline chinh:

A. INPUT AUDIO
- Thu am va phat hien im lang/noi: src/audio/recorder.py
- Chuyen audio sang van ban (ASR): src/recognition/speech_recognizer.py

B. NLP + HIEU Y DINH
- Phan loai y dinh (intent): src/nlp/nlp_model.py
- Trich xuat thuc the (entity): src/nlp/nlp_model.py + src/nlp/entity_extractor.py

C. QUYET DINH DIEU HUONG
- Neu la hoi thoai: src/llm/conversation_engine.py
- Neu la lenh he thong/app/web: src/main.py -> src/actions/*

D. THUC THI + PHAN HOI
- Thuc thi hanh dong (mo app, search web, volume...): src/actions/*
- Phan hoi bang giong noi (TTS): src/audio/speech_synthesizer.py

E. HOC TU DU LIEU NGUOI DUNG
- Ho so va lich su lenh: src/components/user/user_profile.py
- Feedback danh gia: src/components/feedback/feedback_collector.py

============================================================
2) LUONG XU LY CHINH KHI CHAY CHUONG TRINH
============================================================
Diem vao: src/main.py (ham main)

Buoc 1: Khoi tao thanh phan
- Tao NLPProcessor, UserProfile, FeedbackCollector.
- Tao SpeechSynthesizer (gTTS, ngon ngu vi).
- Tao ConversationEngine (co the ket noi Ollama neu co).
- Tao Recorder va SpeechRecognizer.

Buoc 2: Lang nghe audio lien tuc
- Recorder bat stream, doc frame audio theo chunk.
- Tinh RMS de xac dinh speech/silence.
- Khi het noi (silence_counter vuot nguong), dung thu.

Buoc 3: Nhan dang giong noi
- SpeechRecognizer.recognize_speech_from_data(audio_data)
- Engine uu tien cau hinh (google/whisper).
- Tra ve text hoac thong bao loi "Could not understand...".

Buoc 4: Tach nhieu lenh trong 1 cau
- execute_commands_advanced() thu dung NLTK sentence tokenizer.
- Neu khong co NLTK hoac 1 cau: fallback execute_commands().
- execute_commands() tach bang cac separator: "va", "and", "sau do", ",", ";".

Buoc 5: Xu ly tung lenh (execute_command)
- Chuyen text thanh lower-case.
- Goi nlp_processor.classify_intent(text).
- Re nhanh:
  + Nhom hoi thoai: conversation/greeting/question_answering/small_talk/help...
  + Nhom hanh dong: open_app, close_app, system_volume, open_website, web_search...

Buoc 6A: Neu la hoi thoai
- conversation_engine.generate_response(text, intent)
- Neu co Ollama va intent phu hop -> sinh cau tra loi bang LLM.
- Neu khong -> PatternMatcher fallback.
- Cap nhat context hoi thoai: conversation_mode, last_command, topic...

Buoc 6B: Neu la lenh hanh dong
- Trich xuat entity (ten app/site/query).
- Goi ham tu actions:
  + open_application / close_application
  + control_volume / control_brightness / shutdown / restart
  + search_web / open_website / search_on_specific_site / youtube search
- Nhan ket qua string de phan hoi nguoi dung.

Buoc 7: Phan hoi bang giong noi
- speech_synthesizer.speak(response)
- Hang doi voice duoc xu ly bat dong bo trong thread rieng.

Buoc 8: Thu feedback dinh ky
- Cu moi 10 lenh, he thong hoi muc do hai long 1-5.
- Feedback ghi vao user_feedback.jsonl.

============================================================
3) CHI TIET "TRUY XUAT DU LIEU" (DATA RETRIEVAL)
============================================================
3.1. Truy xuat du lieu tu JSON cau hinh
Nguon du lieu chinh:
- src/components/data/applications.json
- src/components/data/websites.json
- src/components/data/conversation.json
- src/components/data/search_engines.json

Lop truy xuat:
- src/utils/data_loader.py (DataLoader)

Du lieu duoc lay ra de lam gi:
- get_app_keywords(): map tu khoa -> app.
- get_website_keywords(): map tu khoa -> website.
- get_search_url(site): template URL tim kiem tung trang.
- get_app_commands()/get_site_commands(): bo tu khoa dieu khien.

3.2. Truy xuat du lieu mo hinh AI
- NLP model cache trong src/models/.
- NLPProcessor tai tokenizer/model tu Hugging Face (PhoBERT + NER pipeline).
- Neu model loi/khong co, he thong fallback sang rule-based.

3.3. Truy xuat du lieu ngu canh hoi thoai
- ConversationEngine giu conversation_history (bo nho tam).
- current_context luu topic, last_command, preferences.
- Co timeout xoa context khi khong hoat dong qua lau.

3.4. Truy xuat du lieu nguoi dung
- Profile: src/components/user/user_data/<user>_profile.json
- History: src/components/user/user_data/<user>_commands.json
- Feedback: src/components/feedback/feedback_data/user_feedback.jsonl

============================================================
4) CHI TIET "PHAN TICH" (ANALYSIS)
============================================================
4.1. Phan tich intent
- NLPProcessor.classify_intent() dung keyword scoring + regex pattern.
- Co nhom intent dac biet nhu search_on_site, open_website, youtube_search.
- Neu khong khop -> "unknown".

4.2. Phan tich entity
- Truoc tien thu model NER.
- Neu that bai/khong phu hop -> fallback rule-based extract_entity().
- Entity tieu bieu: app_name, website_name, search query, site.

4.3. Phan tich hoi thoai
- ConversationEngine quyet dinh dung LLM hay PatternMatcher.
- OllamaClient tao prompt theo context + intent + user_input.
- PatternMatcher dung regex + intent-based response + context-aware response.

4.4. Phan tich chat luong qua feedback
- Collect score hai long va thong tin du doan intent/entity.
- update_from_feedback() co the luu them training point + bo sung intent mapping.

============================================================
5) LUONG DU LIEU TOM TAT (END-TO-END)
============================================================
Audio (microphone)
-> Recorder (frame/chunk, silence detection)
-> SpeechRecognizer (ASR)
-> Text command
-> NLPProcessor (intent + entity)
-> Router (conversation vs command action)
-> (LLM/Pattern) hoac (actions/*)
-> Response text
-> SpeechSynthesizer (TTS)
-> Audio response cho nguoi dung
-> (optional) Feedback + update profile/data

============================================================
6) CAC DIEM FALLBACK VA CHONG LOI
============================================================
- Whisper khong co -> fallback Google ASR.
- Ollama khong ket noi duoc -> fallback PatternMatcher.
- NER loi -> fallback rule-based entity extractor.
- NLTK khong co -> fallback tach lenh don gian.
- website/app khong nam trong map -> thu tao URL/app command generic.

============================================================

Nhan audio -> hieu ngon ngu -> ra quyet dinh -> thuc thi hanh dong/hoi thoai -> phan hoi bang giong noi,
kem co che luu lich su, thu feedback va fallback de dam bao do on dinh khi mot thanh phan AI bi loi.
